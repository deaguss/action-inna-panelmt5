# ActionInna 1.1.0

ActionInna is a risk trade panel for **MetaTrader 5 desktop**, modelled on the order form of the
Exness web terminal. It works out the lot size from the risk you set, previews the order on the
chart, and sends it only when you confirm. This release adds changes to open trades on a confirm
card, slippage handling, support for any MT5 broker, a browser side panel next to TradingView, and
a one-click install.

## What's new

- **Change open trades with a confirm card.** Drag the SL, TP or a pending order's entry of an open
  trade, and a card shows the new prices, the money and % at each level, and the risk : reward.
  Nothing changes until you press Confirm. A pending entry dragged across the market turns the
  order into the other type (a Buy Limit becomes a Buy Stop). A position's stop may go to
  break-even. MT5's own trade lines, which can change an order the moment a drag ends, are hidden
  while ActionInna runs.
- **Slippage handled the way you set the stop.** When a market order fills away from its planned
  price, an SL in pips (and a TP in pips or R) moves with the fill, so the money at risk stays as
  planned. A level set as a price stays. The input "When a market order fills at another price"
  chooses.
- **Any MT5 broker:** filling, expiry and execution modes, netting accounts, accounts without
  opposite positions or with first-in-first-out closes, symbol names with prefixes and suffixes,
  and USD, EUR, JPY and cent accounts.
- **A browser side panel** for Chrome, Edge and Brave, next to TradingView. It has the chart
  panel's order form, numbers and checks, and sends everything through your own MT5, by way of a
  small program on your PC (the link). The symbol follows TradingView's chart. The order and your
  open trades show as lines on TradingView's chart, and dragging them changes the panel. Nothing
  leaves your PC. The panel is not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it is set up from the source
  code (see Install).
- **Confirm sends what you saw.** On the chart and in the browser, Confirm refuses an order whose
  type or lots changed since the preview was drawn, or whose risk grew more than 5%, and shows the
  new numbers first.
- **One-click install** from the source code: `install.bat`.

## Install

**The chart panel only**

1. Download `ActionInna.ex5` from the assets below.
2. In MT5: **File > Open Data Folder**, then open `MQL5\Experts` and copy the file there.
3. In the Navigator, right-click **Expert Advisors** and choose **Refresh**.
4. Turn on **Algo Trading**, drag **ActionInna** onto a chart, and tick **Allow Algo Trading** in the
   Common tab.

**Everything, with the browser side panel**

1. Download **Source code (zip)** below and unzip it to a folder you will keep. MT5 must have been
   opened at least once.
2. Double-click `install.bat`. It compiles ActionInna with the MetaEditor that comes with MT5,
   copies it into every MT5 on this PC, and sets up the link. No administrator rights.
3. In Chrome (or Edge, Brave): open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, press
   **Load unpacked** and pick the folder's `extension` folder.
4. In MT5: **Navigator > Refresh**, put ActionInna on one chart and turn on **Algo Trading**. Then
   open the side panel from the toolbar on a TradingView tab.

**From 1.0.0:** replace `ActionInna.ex5`, then restart MT5 or drag ActionInna onto its charts
again: a chart keeps running the old code until then. Saved settings and recalled SL/TP keep
working. The pip convention "Exness" is now called "10 points".

Start on a **demo account**. The README has a test checklist (section 10) and every input explained
(section 8).

## Safe defaults

- A stop loss and a take profit are required, and both are sent with the order itself.
- An order that risks more than 5% of equity is refused, and so is a change to an open trade that
  raises its risk above that (input "Safety cap"). Tightening a stop is never blocked.
- Changes to open trades go through the confirm card (input "Drag open trades with a confirm
  pop-up", on).
- One-click trading is off, and the browser panel never offers it: Confirm is always a second press
  there.
- Magic number 0, so trades count as manual trades for other tools on the account.

## What was tested

- Compiles with 0 errors and 0 warnings in MetaEditor (MT5 desktop for Windows).
- Tried by hand in MT5 desktop:
  - a pending SELL STOP was placed through the panel, with its SL and TP;
  - Pin to new charts works, and so does reopening MT5 with the panel back on its charts;
  - the risk numbers on the panel match a hand calculation;
  - an open position's SL and TP were changed by dragging their lines and confirming on the card.
- Without MT5, the native tests run the panel's own code against a fake MT5 whose server applies
  each of nine broker set-ups' rules: about 3.6 million checks per run, and every request the panel
  sends is checked against that server. The browser side panel ran against a stand-in for the link
  and MT5 (about 816,000 checks), and the link against made-up EAs (about 26,000). In MT5 only the
  link's named pipe has run, with a test EA: a round trip from the browser to MT5 and back took
  31 ms (median).

Not tried yet:
- market orders through the panel, and dragging a pending order's entry;
- the browser side panel with the real EA in MT5;
- the rest of the checklist (README section 10);
- brokers other than Exness, and MT5 on Mac or Linux.

Reports are welcome in the issue tracker. Report wrong or unexpected orders privately (SECURITY.md).

## Known limitations

- The side panel is loaded unpacked until it is in the Chrome Web Store. Its ID comes from a public
  key, so while it is set up this way, load unpacked only extensions you trust (SECURITY.md).
- The lines on TradingView's chart use TradingView's internal chart code, as TradingView offers no
  public way to draw on its charts. If TradingView changes that code, the lines stop showing and the
  panel works as before. They stand at your broker's prices, which can differ a little from
  TradingView's data.
- Typing a value into a field on the chart needs a click on it: MT5 offers no way for a program to
  focus a field.
- The Strategy Tester sends no clicks, so the panel stays idle there.
- On a netting account, an order merges with the open position and replaces its SL/TP (the panel
  warns).
