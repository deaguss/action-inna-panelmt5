# ActionInna 1.0.0

First public release. ActionInna is a risk trade panel for **MetaTrader 5 desktop**, modelled on the
order form of the Exness web terminal. It sits on the chart in place of MT5's One-Click panel.

## What it does

- **Risk presets** 1% / 1.5% / 2% plus a custom value in %, money or fixed lots. The lot size is worked
  out from the stop and rounded down, so the risk never goes above the amount you set.
- **Stop loss in pips or price, take profit in pips, price or R** (a multiple of the stop).
- **A preview before anything is sent:** entry, SL and TP lines on the chart with their price, pips,
  money and %. Drag them and every number follows. Nothing goes out until you press CONFIRM.
- **Market and pending orders.** Drag the entry line, or type a price, and the panel picks Limit or Stop.
- **Recall of earlier SL/TP** per symbol, stored as price distances so they mean the same at any broker.
- **Hotkeys** for preview, risk, confirm and cancel, plus Tab / Shift+Tab through every control.
- A **slim layout** for short or tiled charts, and **dark and light themes** that follow the chart.
- **Pin to new charts**, so every chart you open loads the panel.

## Install

1. Download `ActionInna.ex5` from the assets below.
2. In MT5: **File > Open Data Folder**, then open `MQL5\Experts` and copy the file there.
3. In the Navigator, right-click **Expert Advisors** and choose **Refresh**.
4. Turn on **Algo Trading**, drag **ActionInna** onto a chart, and tick **Allow Algo Trading** in the
   Common tab.

Start on a **demo account**. The README has a test checklist (section 10) and every input explained
(section 8). To build from source, see README section 1.

## Safe defaults

- A stop loss and a take profit are required, and both are sent with the order itself.
- An order risking more than 5% of equity is refused (input "Safety cap").
- One-click trading is off; turning it on asks first.
- Magic number 0, so trades count as manual trades for other tools on the account.

## What was tested

- Compiles with 0 errors and 0 warnings in MetaEditor (MT5 desktop for Windows).
- Tried by hand in MT5 desktop:
  - a pending SELL STOP was placed through the panel, with its SL and TP;
  - Pin to new charts works, and so does reopening MT5 with the panel restored;
  - the risk numbers on the panel match a hand calculation.
- The repository includes `ActionInnaTests`: worked examples plus about 92,000 generated scenarios per
  run, including 2,000 full order plans with live prices.

Not tried yet:
- market orders through the panel;
- the rest of the checklist;
- other brokers;
- MT5 on Mac or Linux.

Reports are welcome in the issue tracker. Report wrong or unexpected orders privately (SECURITY.md).

## Known limitations

- Typing a value into a field needs a click on it: MT5 offers no way for a program to focus a field.
- The Strategy Tester sends no clicks, so the panel stays idle there.
- In the default "Trader" pip convention, a 2-digit crypto symbol counts one point per pip (BTCUSD:
  $0.01). Switch the input to the Exness convention on crypto charts if you prefer bigger pips. Lots and
  money are not affected.
- On a netting account, an order merges with the open position and replaces its SL/TP (the panel warns).
- A rebuilt EA keeps running its old code on open charts until MT5 restarts or it is attached again.
- While you drag a line, MT5 may update the numbers only when you release it.
